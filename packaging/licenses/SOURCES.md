assign a license to Vole's original code.

`MIT.txt`, `Zlib.txt`, `MPL-2.0.txt`, `CC0-1.0.txt`, and
`LGPL-2.1-or-later.txt` are unmodified SPDX License List 3.27.0 texts retrieved
from https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data/tree/v3.27.0/text.
They accompany original published crate archives when the crate omits a
separate notice file. The original archive preserves its package declaration,
authors and source copyright headers. License alternatives are recorded as
declared by upstream; including a text is not a new license choice for Vole.
